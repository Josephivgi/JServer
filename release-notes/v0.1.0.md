First release.

- Watches every host in your ~/.ssh/config over SSH, including hosts behind a ProxyJump.
- Shows CPU, memory, disk, swap, load, network and disk I/O for each server, with history up to 30 days.
- Explains what uses each server's memory and CPU, grouped by VM, container, service or program.
- Lists your SSH keys, which hosts use them, and problems to fix.
- Keeps watching in the background after you close the window. Tick Start with Windows to keep it running after a restart.
- When a server cannot be reached, its card shows why and a Retry button. JServer does not keep retrying on its own.
